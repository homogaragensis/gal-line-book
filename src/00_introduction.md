1. Take a ridicoulus idea and turn it into a real commercial game.
2. Make its source and development completely open from the start (except for the artwork).
3. Write an in-depth tutorial book on web game development.

## The Game
**GAL-LINE** is a pun in Italian, making use of the english word *line* and the italian word *galline*, meaning hens or chickens.
The setting of the game is pretty zany: you embody a chicken, walking in a train station, that fights people crossing the yellow line, making them step back.

The visual concept is drastically simple: a parallax background on a 2D scene, the chicken running and these unknown legs that continuosly block your way.
The gameplay idea is that of an arcade boss rush, where you spam your attacks against the current boss and proceed in the gameplay loop.

Adrenaline is the fuel of the gameplay, and humour is what should keep you unlocking characters.
